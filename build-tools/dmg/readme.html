Install 187N Agent HQ

Three steps, about ten minutes including setup. You need macOS 13 or newer and the licence key you received from 187N.

1

Drag the app to Applications

In this window, drag 187N Agent HQ onto the Applications folder. Then eject the disk.

2

Open it the first time

Open 187N Agent HQ from Applications or Spotlight (⌘ Space, then type “187N”). macOS asks you to confirm an app from outside the App Store. You do this only once:

macOS 15 or newer
Click Done in the message, then open
System Settings → Privacy & Security
Scroll down to “187N Agent HQ was blocked…”, click Open Anyway, enter your Mac password, then Open.

macOS 13 or 14
Control-click (right-click) the app in Applications, choose Open, then Open again.

3

Follow Setup

Setup opens by itself: paste your licence key, then connect your AI tools (Claude Code, Codex or Hermes Agent) with your own subscription. Click Install or Sign in: Terminal opens the official step, and the app turns green by itself when you are done.

Then, each optional and one click: your apps (Composio), more tools (any MCP server, keys, skills), your social accounts (Zernio), your second brain (your Obsidian vault is found for you, or start a new one), Voice (your ElevenLabs key) and Research (your Apify key; every paid run asks first).

You can change everything later from the menu 187N Agent HQ → Setup… (⌘ ,). After an update, macOS may ask once for your Mac password so the new version can read your saved keys (choose Always Allow), and once for the microphone when you use Voice.
